## bAIsect

**Find the right engineer to fix the right problems.**

bAIsect is a marketplace that connects people building software with AI tools to
human engineers who can diagnose, fix, review, refactor, or finish the parts AI
cannot. You describe the problem in plain English; bAIsect organizes the symptoms,
context and evidence into a clear Issue Report, then matches you with a relevant
specialist.
